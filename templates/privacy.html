Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Spendly is a personal expense tracking app, and we aim to collect only what is needed to provide the service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, and the financial records you enter into the app, including expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type and the pages you visit within Spendly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate your account, store and display your expense data, generate summaries and category breakdowns, respond to support requests, and keep the service secure. We do not use your financial records for advertising.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured servers, and we use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and limit access to it.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who help us run Spendly, such as hosting and infrastructure providers, and only to the extent needed to perform those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the service.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Spendly uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. We may use limited analytics to understand how the service is used so we can improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect how the app works.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can view and update your account information at any time. You may request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and associated records. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information, and you can contact us to exercise them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of Spendly after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
